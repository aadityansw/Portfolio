---

## 📌 Executive Summary
A digital property showroom and content management platform built for **Sai Shree Balajee Homes**, presenting residential properties with architectural elegance while providing an intuitive admin portal for sales agents to update prices and unit availability.

---

## 🎯 The Challenge
Luxury residential buyers evaluate floor plans and finishes in high resolution. However, multi-megabyte architectural photos frequently slow down mobile load times on cellular connections, causing lead drop-off. Furthermore, agents required an easy way to mark units as "Reserved" or "Sold" without touching code.

---

## 💡 The Solution
1. **Adaptive Image Compression:** Automated responsive image pipeline delivering crisp WebP/AVIF images with minimal payload.
2. **Interactive Floorplans:** Interactive modals allowing buyers to inspect dimensions, balcony views, and square footages.
3. **Instant WhatsApp Enquiries:** Pre-populated property interest messages directly connected with assigned brokers.
4. **Custom Admin Portal:** Simple, secure administrative dashboard for updating unit statuses in seconds.

---

## 🚀 Key Results
- 98+ Google Lighthouse Performance score.
- Over 70% reduction in image payload size without loss of visual sharpness.
- Under 60-second lead contact turnaround through direct WhatsApp integration.
