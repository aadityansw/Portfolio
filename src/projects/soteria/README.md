**Category:** UI/UX Design & Product Strategy

**Role:** Lead Product & Interaction Designer

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
A comprehensive product design study that reinvents the hotel booking journey for the mobile generation, replacing clinical OTA tables with immersive video reviews, map-based neighborhood clustering, and a friction-free 3-tap checkout.

---

## 🎯 The Problem
Legacy Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) suffer from severe mobile abandonment rates:
- Overwhelming screens with aggressive upselling popups.
- Up to 7 separate friction-heavy form screens before payment confirmation.
- Stock marketing photography that doesn't reflect actual room conditions.

---

## 💡 The Solution
1. **Creator-Led Authenticity:** Integrating short-form vertical video clips from verified guests into hotel property listings.
2. **Simplified 3-Tap Checkout:** Pre-stored preferences, Apple Pay / Google Pay support, reducing booking time from 4 minutes to 30 seconds.
3. **Spatial Map Discovery:** Visual exploration with real-time neighborhood pricing and walking distance markers.
4. **Cohesive Design System:** Modular 40+ artboard component library ready for production implementation.
