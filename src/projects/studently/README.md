# Studently — Centralized Academic Hub & Student Portal

**Category:** Flutter Apps / EdTech  
**Timeline:** 2022 — 2025  
**Active Audience:** 2,000+ College Students  
**Role:** Lead Product Engineer & Mobile Developer  
**Stack:** Flutter, Dart, SQLite, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Figma  

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
**Studently** is a student portal application built with Flutter that unifies timetable updates, exam notices, and assignment tracking into one streamlined experience for college students.

---

## 🎯 The Problem
University communication is notoriously fragmented:
- Timetable cancellations announced in noisy messaging chats.
- Critical exam dates posted on physical departmental bulletin boards.
- Assignment guidelines lost across scattered email threads.
Students frequently missed room reassignments and submission deadlines due to inconsistent information distribution.

---

## 💡 The Solution
Studently replaces bulletin boards and chat clutter with an authoritative, offline-first mobile portal:
1. **At-A-Glance Schedule:** Displays the current and upcoming lectures, room numbers, and faculty names with live cancellation badges.
2. **Offline-First Persistence:** SQLite database caches timetables locally so students can check class locations even inside basement laboratories without Wi-Fi.
3. **Smart Reminders:** Push notifications sent 24 hours and 2 hours before assignment deadlines.
4. **Attendance Health Ring:** Visual dashboard showing attendance percentages and alerting when approaching mandatory criteria.

---

- [x] Assignment Due-Date Tracker with Document Links
- [x] Attendance Percentage Ring & Minimum Criterion Alerts
